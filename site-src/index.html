Tokk

Tokk lets a web page react to short sounds, such as a clap, a knock or a tongue click, with the sound model and every recording kept on your device.

Audio stays on your device. Each page below asks for the microphone only after you press its button.

  • Teach a sound: a developer page that records a sound ten times and writes a sound model.
  • Hello example: listen to the microphone and read every sound and pattern as text.
  • Hands-Free: browse the web with mouth sounds.
  • Knock Once for Yes: answer a ghost's questions by knocking.

Teach a sound

A page for developers. It records a sound ten times, saves the recordings as wav files in your project folder, and writes sounds.json. Recordings stay on this computer.

Folder

Pick the folder that holds sounds/ and sounds.json, for example examples/hello. The page writes sounds/<name>/<name>-NN.wav and sounds.json there and nowhere else.

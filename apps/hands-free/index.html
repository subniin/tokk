Hands-Free reader

Your audio never leaves this device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

When the microphone is on, it stays on while this page is open, even when listening is off, so that three quick clicks can turn listening on again. Closing or reloading the page stops it.

Read with mouth sounds

This page is a small reading site that you can use without your hands. A frame moves over the links, and a tongue click presses the link that was framed when you clicked. A hum that rises or falls scrolls the page.

The microphone asks for permission only after you press the first button. You teach three sounds first, which takes about a minute. You can also use the keyboard only.

1.2 s

    Heard: nothing yet

    Did: nothing yet

    Highlighted: nothing

    Keyboard

    • K: the same as a click (open the highlighted link)
    • B: the same as two clicks (back to the list)
    • L: the same as three clicks (listening on or off)
    • P: pause or resume the scan
    • T: Teach again
    • Tab, Enter, Space, the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down work as in any page, and scroll the page in place of a hum.
    • Esc closes the teaching window.
    • The scan step slider changes with the arrow keys, Home and End.

Your audio never leaves this device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

When you enter with the microphone, the microphone stays on while this page is open, except for the short moments when a sound effect plays. Closing or reloading the page stops it.

A séance at the table

A spirit waits in a dark room and asks you questions. Knock once on the table for yes, and twice, quickly, for no. To call the spirit, knock, knock, pause, knock.

Long ago the inventor Thomas Edison talked of a machine for speaking with the dead. Tonight a wooden table will have to do.

The page asks for the microphone only after you press the first button. On your first visit you knock ten times so the spirits learn your knock. You can also play with the keyboard and the buttons.

What the room heard and said

    Keyboard

    • Y: yes (the same as one knock)
    • N: no (the same as two knocks)
    • S: summon the spirit (the same as knock, knock, pause, knock)
    • T: Teach again
    • M: sound effects on or off
    • Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and Space work as in any page.
    • Esc closes the teaching window.
